A trusted construction partner built on quality and accountability.

Established in 2025, PT. TNL Construction Indonesia is an integrated construction company delivering reliable solutions for industrial and commercial projects.

We provide comprehensive, end-to-end construction services, covering civil and structural works, mechanical and electrical systems, and interior fit-out. By integrating multiple disciplines under one coordinated approach, we help our clients achieve efficient project execution, consistent quality, and dependable results.

Our commitment to quality, safety, accountability, and professional project management is at the core of everything we do. We work closely with clients, consultants, and project partners to deliver solutions that meet technical requirements, project schedules, and the highest standards of workmanship.

At PT. TNL Construction Indonesia, we aim to be more than a contractor. We strive to be a trusted construction partner, delivering projects with integrity, precision, and long-term value.
